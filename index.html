Private personal integration

Jeremy OpenClaw

A single-user personal assistant integration connecting an owner-controlled OpenClaw installation with Google Workspace.

What it does

Jeremy OpenClaw helps its sole user work with Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides. It performs actions only in response to that user’s requests.

Who can use it

This is not a public service and does not accept registrations. It is operated for personal use by its owner.

How data is handled

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner-controlled OpenClaw host. Google data may be processed by the owner’s configured OpenClaw runtime and AI providers only as needed to carry out requested tasks. It is not sold or used for advertising.
