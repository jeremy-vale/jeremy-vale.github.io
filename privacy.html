Jeremy OpenClaw

Privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026

Scope

Jeremy OpenClaw is a private, single-user personal assistant integration. It is not offered to the public and has no user-registration system.

Google data accessed

With the owner’s authorization, the integration may access Gmail messages, Google Calendar events, and files in Google Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides. Access is used only to carry out requests made by the authorized owner.

Use and sharing

Google data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared for unrelated purposes. It may be processed by the owner’s configured OpenClaw runtime and AI service providers only when necessary to fulfil the owner’s requests.

Jeremy OpenClaw’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner-controlled OpenClaw host. Task outputs, logs or workspace files may contain information selected by the owner and remain under the owner’s control. The public website does not collect or store Google account data.

Security and control

Access is protected through Google OAuth. The owner can revoke access at any time from the Google Account permissions page and can delete locally stored credentials and retained task data.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
